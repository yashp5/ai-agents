# HomeShield Homeowners Policy — Coverage Terms (HS-STD-2026)

## Water damage coverage

Sudden and accidental discharge of water from plumbing, heating, or air
conditioning systems is a covered peril. This includes burst pipes, failed
supply lines, and water heater ruptures. Resulting damage to floors, walls,
and built-in fixtures is covered under Dwelling (Coverage A).

Damage caused by gradual leaks, seepage, or deferred maintenance is NOT
covered. If water escaped continuously over 14 days or more, the loss is
excluded regardless of when it was discovered.

## Personal property

Personal property (rugs, furniture, electronics, clothing) damaged by a
covered peril is covered under Coverage C, up to a limit of $5,000 per
incident. Items are reimbursed at actual cash value (purchase price less
depreciation) unless the Replacement Cost endorsement was purchased.

## Deductible

A deductible of $500 applies to each claim. The deductible is subtracted
from the approved claim amount, not from each damaged item separately.

## Mold

Mold remediation is excluded, except when the mold results directly from a
covered sudden water loss AND the policyholder took reasonable steps to dry
the affected area within 72 hours. In that case remediation is covered up
to $2,500.

## Exclusions summary

Flood (rising external water), earthquake, intentional damage, and losses
during periods when the home was vacant for more than 60 consecutive days
are excluded. Flood coverage requires a separate policy through the
National Flood Insurance Program.
