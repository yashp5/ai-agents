# HomeShield — Customer FAQ

## Will filing a claim raise my premium?

Filing a claim can affect your renewal premium. A single weather-related or
sudden-loss claim typically has a small impact; multiple claims within 3
years usually lead to a larger increase or non-renewal.

## Can I choose my own contractor?

Yes. HomeShield never requires you to use a specific contractor. We pay
based on the approved estimate; if your chosen contractor charges more than
comparable local rates, the difference may not be covered.

## How do I cancel my policy?

You can cancel anytime with written notice. Unearned premium is refunded
pro-rata. Note that a lapse in coverage can affect your rates with any
future insurer.

## Do you cover home offices?

Business equipment in a home office is covered only up to $1,500 under the
standard policy. If you run a business from home, ask about the Home
Business endorsement.
