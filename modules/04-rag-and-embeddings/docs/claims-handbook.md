# HomeShield Claims Handbook — Internal Process Guide

## Prompt notice requirement

Policyholders must report a loss within 60 days of discovering it. Claims
reported later may be denied for late notice unless the delay was outside
the policyholder's control (hospitalization, extended travel with no
ability to communicate, etc.). Document the reason for any delay.

## Required documentation

Every claim needs: (1) a written repair estimate or invoice from a licensed
contractor, (2) photos of the damage, and (3) proof of ownership or value
for personal property items over $250 (receipt, credit card statement, or
photo of the item before the loss). Verbal quotes are not sufficient for
payment — request the written version before adjudication.

## Approval authority

Claims with an approved amount under $3,000 can be settled by any licensed
adjuster. Amounts from $3,000 to $25,000 require senior adjuster sign-off.
Above $25,000, regional claims manager approval is required.

## Payment timeline

Once approved, payment is issued within 5 business days by ACH if the
policyholder has banking details on file, otherwise by check within 10
business days. State regulations in some jurisdictions require faster
payment; check the state supplement.
